Client Acquisitions

Executive Rule: 180-Day Rule

One record per acquired (non-internal) client. The salesperson earns the stored rate on every paid income of that client's client projects whose start date is on/after the acquisition date and before the exclusive stop date (acquisition date + 180 days) — including projects added later. Ordinary Close commission for that client is then suppressed.
